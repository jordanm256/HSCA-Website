A-101

Why the drawing matters

General notes

A kitchen or bathroom is a small room full of expensive decisions. We make those decisions on paper first, with you, where changing your mind costs an eraser instead of a wall.

  1. Measured, not guessed

    We survey the room to the millimetre and record every pipe, socket, beam and fall in the floor. The design starts from the room you actually have.

  2. A drawing you can price

    Our drawings list every material and fitting. The quote is built from that list, so you can see what each choice costs and change it before work starts.

  3. Designed for Kampala

    Water pressure that comes and goes, power cuts, two rainy seasons, red dust. We plan for tanks and pumps, gas and electric cooking, air that moves, and finishes that clean easily.

A-301

From first visit to handed-over keys

Section A–A
  1. 01

    Consultation

    We visit, listen and measure. You tell us how the room should work; we tell you what the room allows.

    You get a measured survey and written notes.

  2. 02

    Design

    We draw options in plan, elevation and 3D, with a materials board you can hold.

    You get drawings you keep, whoever builds.

  3. 03

    Costing

    An itemised quote taken straight from the drawings. Swap a tile or a tap and see the price move.

    You decide nothing starts until you sign off.

  4. 04

    Build

    Our team builds to the drawings, in order: services first, finishes last. You get photo updates on WhatsApp.

    You see progress without standing on site.

  5. 05

    Handover

    A walk-through, a closed snag list, and care notes for every finish we used.

A-601

The practice

Two founders, one drawing board

Hearth and spire: the warm centre of a home and the structure that lifts it. We use both words on purpose.

HSCA was started by two people with different jobs. One leads design and keeps us connected to the building industry. The other runs client work and operations in Kampala, so there is always someone you can call who knows your job.

We are small by choice for now. It means the person who draws your room is the person who checks it is built right.

Quality assurance
Drawings are checked before they are priced, and work is checked against the drawings before we hand it over.
Innovation
Old techniques and new methods side by side, chosen for the job rather than for fashion.
Mutual growth
Fair terms for clients, fundis and suppliers. We want to work with the same good people for years.
